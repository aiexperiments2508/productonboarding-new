# New Line Onboarding - What "Complete" Means

The rule a new starter is told on their first week and no system has ever
checked. It is written down here so that it can be.

## The bar

A product is ready to go downstream when every one of the following is true. A
product failing any one of them goes back to the source that owns it, named.

- Every attribute the category defines has a value, or a recorded reason why it
  does not apply.
- Every attribute required by a channel the product will list on has a value.
- The values agree across the sources that sent them, or the disagreement has
  been settled by the precedence in `POL-002` and the losing value withdrawn.
- The imagery required for the category is present.
- No claim is made that the record does not substantiate.
- Nothing in the record is forbidden by a regulation covering the category.

## Imagery

Required by category, not by taste:

- `home.` - a hero shot and at least one in-situ image. Appliances are bought on
  how they look in a room, and a cut-out alone converts badly enough that
  category management treats its absence as an incomplete record.
- `food.` - a pack front, plus a legible image of the ingredient panel. The
  panel image is not decoration: it is what a shopper with an allergy reads
  when they do not trust the transcription.
- `audio.` - a hero shot. In-ear products photograph poorly in situ and the
  category has never required it.

## Returning a product to its source

A return names three things: the finding, the attribute or asset it concerns,
and the **system** that supplied it. "The data is incomplete" is not a return;
"the GDSN pool sent net content in its own vocabulary and the field is still
empty" is one, because somebody can act on it.

Return to the system that supplied the value, not to the supplier. A supplier
whose portal entry was fine and whose data pool record was not is not at fault
for the second one, and telling them so wastes the correction.

## What is not a reason to hold a product

- Copy that could be better. Marketing quality is not completeness.
- A missing optional attribute the category does not define as applicable.
- A disagreement already settled by precedence, where the losing value has been
  withdrawn and recorded as withdrawn.
