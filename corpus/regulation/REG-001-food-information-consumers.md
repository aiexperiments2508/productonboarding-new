# Food Information to Consumers - Mandatory Particulars

This is the requirement, not our reading of it. `POL-001` is the retailer's
allergen policy and it is written to satisfy this document; where the two are
read together, this one governs. A system that cites its own policy as evidence
of compliance is marking its own homework.

## Scope

Applies to every prepacked food offered for sale to a final consumer. In this
catalogue that is every product whose category begins `food.`.

## Mandatory particulars

A prepacked food SHALL NOT be offered for sale unless all of the following are
present and legible on the listing:

1. The name of the food.
2. The list of ingredients, **in descending order of weight** at the time of
   use. The order is a legal declaration and not a presentation choice; a
   reordered list is a different declaration.
3. Any ingredient causing allergies or intolerances, emphasised within the
   ingredient list.
4. The net quantity.
5. A durability indication.

An omission of any one of these is a bar to sale. It is not a quality defect
and it is not gradeable: the listing is not saleable until the particular is
present.

## Allergen declaration

The fourteen regulated allergens SHALL be declared where present as an
ingredient or as a processing aid. Where cross-contamination cannot be excluded,
a precautionary statement is permitted and, once made, carries the same weight
as a positive declaration.

**A precautionary statement is not a softer form of a declaration.** A product
carrying "may contain peanuts" is a product about which a peanut-free claim may
not be made, in any channel, in any wording.

## Claims that this document forbids

A claim of absence - "free from", "no added", "suitable for" - SHALL NOT be made
where the corresponding allergen appears in either the ingredient list or a
precautionary statement. This is absolute and admits no confidence threshold: an
absence claim is a statement about what is not there, and uncertainty about what
is there is uncertainty about the claim.

## Saleability

Where a mandatory particular is absent, or an absence claim contradicts a
declaration, the product is **not saleable**. This is a different finding from a
content defect: a missing bullet point costs a shopper information, and a
missing allergen declaration is an offence.
