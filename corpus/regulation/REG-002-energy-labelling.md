# Energy Labelling and Declared Performance

## Scope

Household appliances placed on the market, which in this catalogue is every
product whose category begins `home.`.

## Declared values

A declared performance figure - rated power, sound level, coverage area - SHALL
correspond to the value in the technical documentation for **that model**, not
for the range it belongs to. A figure measured on one model and published
against another is a misdeclaration whether or not the difference is material.

This is the requirement behind the base-versus-variant distinction. Where a
document asserts a figure for a range and the range's models differ, the figure
may not be published against a model the measurement did not cover.

## Energy class

Where an energy class is published it SHALL be derived from the declared
consumption. A class that does not follow from the wattage on the same listing
is an inconsistency the listing may not carry.

## Efficiency claims

A comparative or superlative efficiency claim - "low energy", "most efficient",
"uses less power than" - SHALL be substantiated against the declared figure at
the time the claim is published. A claim that was true of a superseded figure
is not true of the listing carrying the corrected one.

## Saleability

A listing carrying a declared figure that the technical documentation does not
support is **not saleable** until the figure is corrected or withdrawn. A
listing carrying an unsubstantiated efficiency claim may be sold once the claim
is removed; the claim is severable and the figure is not.
