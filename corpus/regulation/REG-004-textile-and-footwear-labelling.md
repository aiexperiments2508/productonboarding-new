# Textile Fibre Composition and Footwear Material Labelling

## Scope

Every product whose category begins `apparel.`, and household textiles under
`home.textiles.`. A product made of textile is in scope whether it is worn or
not; a towel and a jumper are labelled the same way.

## Fibre composition

A textile product SHALL NOT be offered for sale unless its fibre composition is
declared.

The declaration names each fibre and its percentage by weight, **in descending
order of percentage**. The order is part of the declaration and not a
presentation choice - "60% Cotton, 40% Polyester" and "40% Polyester, 60%
Cotton" are the same fibres and different declarations, and only the first is
correct.

This is the same rule `REG-001` applies to an ingredient list, for the same
reason, and it is enforced the same way: `textile.fibre_composition` is an
**ordered** attribute, and a reordering is a change rather than a rewording.

Percentages SHALL total 100. A fibre present at 5% or less may be declared as
"other fibres" only where it is not the sole content of a functional component.

## Care instructions

A care code SHALL accompany the composition. It is not a mandatory particular
under this regulation and its absence does not bar sale; it is required by
`INT-001` before a line may launch, which is the retailer's own bar and a
higher one.

## Country of origin

Where an origin is stated, it SHALL be the country where the last substantial
transformation took place - which for a garment is where it was made up, not
where the fabric was woven and not where the yarn was spun.

Origin is a **restricted claim** under `INT-002`: it may be published only with
substantiation on file. A change of origin therefore does two things at once -
it changes a labelling particular, and it invalidates every claim resting on
the old one until new substantiation is held.

## Children's nightwear

Nightwear intended for children SHALL carry either a flammability performance
statement or the prescribed warning. Neither is optional and neither may be
inferred: a garment in `apparel.kids.sleepwear` with no flammability
declaration on file is not saleable, and the correct response is to return it
to the supplier rather than to select the safer-sounding of the two.

## Footwear

Footwear SHALL declare the material of the upper, the lining and the outsole.
The three are declared separately; a single material statement covering the
whole shoe does not satisfy this section.
