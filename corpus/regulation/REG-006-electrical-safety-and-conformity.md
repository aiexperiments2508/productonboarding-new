# Electrical Equipment Safety, Supply and Conformity

## Scope

Every product whose category begins `electronics.`, and every mains-powered
product under `home.`. `REG-002` governs the *declared performance* of an
appliance - its rated power, its energy class - and this document governs
whether it may be supplied at all.

## Conformity

Equipment placed on the market SHALL be covered by a declaration of conformity
held by the retailer, identified on the product record by its reference.

**A lapsed declaration is not a safety finding.** The product has not changed
and is not alleged to be unsafe; what has expired is the evidence. So a lapse
does not bar sale under this section. What it does is remove the substantiation
behind every claim that rested on it, and a claim without substantiation is
governed by `INT-002` and is not publishable.

Recording it as a safety issue would be the wrong answer twice over: it would
overstate the risk to a shopper and it would send the supplier chasing a
product problem that does not exist. The supplier owes a certificate.

## Supply and plug type

Equipment supplied with a mains plug SHALL declare the plug type. Equipment
supplied for a different market's socket, or with a conversion adaptor in the
box, SHALL say so on the listing where a shopper reads it and not only in the
specification table.

## Cells and batteries

A product containing a lithium cell SHALL declare the cell type. The
declaration is required whether the cell is user-replaceable or not, and it is
what the carrier reads: a lithium cell changes how a product may be shipped and
stored, and a product record that does not name one is a record the logistics
system cannot act on.

`specs.battery_type` is safety-class for this reason and not because a cell is
dangerous in normal use.

## Energy labelling

Where an energy class is published it SHALL be derived from the declared
consumption and registered. A class that does not follow from the wattage on
the same listing is an inconsistency the listing may not carry - which is
`REG-002`'s rule, restated here because the two documents cover overlapping
products and a reader who found only one of them should not be misled.
