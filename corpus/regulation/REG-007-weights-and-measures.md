# Weights, Measures and Unit Pricing

## Scope

Every prepacked product sold by quantity. In this catalogue that is everything
carrying `pack.net_quantity` - grocery, household and personal care, baby,
health and pet.

## Net quantity

A prepacked product SHALL NOT be offered for sale unless its net quantity is
declared, in a unit appropriate to the product, on the pack and on the listing.

The declared quantity is the quantity at the time of packaging. It is a
mandatory particular: its absence is a bar to sale and not a quality defect,
which is the same character `REG-001` gives the mandatory particulars for food.

## Unit pricing

Where a unit price is displayed it SHALL be derived from the declared net
quantity and the selling price, and it SHALL be displayed in the unit
prescribed for the product class.

This is why a net quantity change is never immaterial. Three things move
together and none of them is copy:

- the declaration on the pack and the listing,
- the shelf-edge label, which prints the quantity,
- the unit price, which is computed from it.

A change propagated to the first and not the other two produces a shelf edge
that contradicts the pack and a unit price that is arithmetically wrong. Both
are offences under this regulation, and neither is visible on the product page
where the change was made.

## A smaller pack at the same price

Reducing the net quantity while holding the price is lawful and is not this
document's concern. What is this document's concern is that the reduction is
declared everywhere the old quantity appears, on the day the new pack reaches
the shelf and not before or after.

Publishing the new quantity early misdescribes stock still on sale. Publishing
it late misdescribes stock that has arrived. Effective dating is therefore not
an administrative nicety here; it is the whole of the obligation.

## Tolerances

An average-quantity system applies. A single pack below the declared quantity
is not automatically an offence; a batch whose average is below it is. Nothing
in the product record can establish either, so this system does not attempt to:
a quantity complaint is referred to the supplier with the batch identified, and
no value in the catalogue is changed on the strength of one pack.

## Related

`REG-001` for food's mandatory particulars, `CHN-005` for the shelf-edge
budget the quantity has to fit in, `POL-002` for materiality - a net quantity
change is material by construction and is never suppressed as a small numeric
move.
